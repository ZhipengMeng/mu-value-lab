SKHY has the highest modeled upside

Base-case judgment: SKHY first, MU second, SNDK third. SKHY combines the lowest recalculated calendar-year P/E with the largest modeled cash-flow value relative to price. Its lead is conditional on recurring earnings, currency and the return required for its risks.

Company forecasts and assumptions are uncertain. These are present values, not 12-month analyst targets or guaranteed returns. All SKHY dollar values are per Nasdaq ADS.

Test the comparison

Interactive controls keep each company’s revenue, product costs, taxes and investment path separate. Base FX is flat. Annual won depreciation changes SKHY’s equivalent KRW discount rate: (1 + USD return) × (1 + FX depreciation) − 1. A common 12% return is a comparison assumption, not a measured WACC.

Put forward P/E on the same calendar

Derived CY2027 proxies, not reported CY consensus or verified next-twelve-month EPS. MU and SNDK are calendarized by month-weighting annual forecasts. This assumes uniform earnings within each fiscal year and ignores 52/53-week differences. Quarterly seasonality and earnings acceleration can change the results.

StockAnalysis/S&P labels EPS as adjusted. Exact adjustments and share assumptions are not harmonized across providers’ company estimates. Recalculated native annual P/Es are SNDK 7.36× (FY27), MU 5.84× (FY27) and SKHY 4.82× (CY27). The SKHY headline 4.17× remains unreconciled and is excluded.

SNDK: the earlier premium was assumption-driven

The preserved v1.5 model produced $2,683–$2,817 at a 12% required return. Its terminal operating margin was 74.50%, with 58–60% of value from the terminal calculation. Management’s 75% target is a FY2028–30 average, not a perpetual margin.

Changing only that terminal margin to 50% gives $2,054–$2,143 in the old model. The new standalone SNDK central case is about $1,998 after also revising the operating horizon, end-market path, JV cash budgets and equity bridge. These revisions are not a single-variable waterfall.

FY2027 model EPS is $215.02 versus $214.82 consensus. FY2028 model EPS is $240.91 versus $266.30 consensus because the central reference applies 75% operating margin. The forecast gap is disclosed; a multi-year average target does not fix an individual year’s margin.

The remaining cash-budget uncertainty matters

Removing the unknown cash allowance can bring SNDK approximately level with MU’s base upside. It does not make SNDK the cheapest of the three. The precise future net JV/working-capital/tax cash split remains unverified.

How robust is the ranking?

SKHY → MU → SNDK holds in all 240 common-parameter combinations tested. The grid varies return 10–16%, terminal margin 30–70%, incremental ROIC 15–35%, and growth 0–3%, while holding each operating path fixed. These are selected sensitivities, not 240 independent observations or a probability estimate.

Price stress applies an extra 20% price-level decline at the start of CY2029 and another at the start of CY2030. Fiscal-year overlaps are month-weighted; costs and shipments retain their own company assumptions. Capital stress scales investment and D&A together. The persistent cash drain fades in during 2035–38, then remains.

SKHY’s lead can disappear under company-specific risk

Holding MU at 12%, SKHY’s modeled upside falls to MU’s level when SKHY’s required KRW return reaches about 14.57% under flat FX. Separately, a 12.8% relative haircut to SKHY’s CY2027 EPS removes its P/E advantage over MU, holding prices and MU EPS fixed. These are different tests, not interchangeable measures.

Company scenarios are deliberately separate

Downside and upside cases differ in business mechanisms and severity. They are not matched probabilities and are not used to assert a risk-adjusted expected return. All three can suffer substantial downside; a low P/E can reflect earnings near a cycle peak.

What is verified, and what is assumed?

Verified / observed

Saved company filings and disclosures support historical balances, cash flows, share/depositary facts and management outlook. The October 9 closes and dated external annual estimates were checked against accessible provider pages. Estimate verification confirms what the provider published, not what the companies will earn.

Assumed / derived

Shipments, pricing, costs, investment schedules, cash normalization, balance-date cash estimates, perpetual economics and FX paths remain assumptions. The central 50% terminal margin is a common control, not proof that these businesses have equal long-run profitability.

All models discount to October 9, 2026, use eight explicit annual forecasts through 2034, four normalization years through 2038, and terminal cash beginning in 2039. Native fiscal payment dates differ and are discounted accordingly. MU and SNDK operate in nominal USD model units; SKHY in nominal KRW, converted to USD per ADS at 1,340.88 in the base case.

The date-aligned P/E proxy is a separate valuation cross-check. DCF values are not forced to match a common P/E. Portfolio gains and customer financing are excluded from operating cash; balance-sheet assets and obligations enter once. SBC remains an economic cost. Future share counts and financing remain uncertain.
